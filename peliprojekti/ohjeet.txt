HOW TO PLAY

Move around the museum to discover new rooms and talk to the museum characters. 

Talk to the characters to gather information and clues about the diamond theft.

Search the rooms to find items that may help you solve the mystery.

check your clues to see what information you have gathered.

When you think you have enough evidence, accuse the person you believe stole the diamond.

choose wisely, as accusing the wrong person will end the game and you will lose the case.
